Write the launch email for our new Aurora X2 wireless headphones.

Product facts: 40-hour battery life; adaptive noise cancelling; a 5-minute
quick charge gives 4 hours of playback; memory-foam ear cushions. Price: $249.
Launch offer: 20% off until 30 November.

Start with a subject line. Then greet the reader by their first name.

Describe exactly three features of the X2. Give each feature its own sentence,
and follow it with a sentence about the benefit to the customer.

Mention the price, $249, once. If you mention the launch offer, say when it
ends in the same sentence.

Do not mention competitors.

Keep the body under 7 sentences.

End with a call to action, followed by the sign-off "The Aurora Team".

Use a warm, confident tone.
